# Faultline workspace

## Register

product

## Users

On-call engineers investigating performance and availability incidents, comparing explanations and reviewing safe interventions.

## Product Purpose

Make an incident investigation inspectable across production, clean clones and replay. The model proposes; measurement decides. This disconnected preview uses simulated data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, investigative.

## Anti-references

No alarm-wall dashboard, decorative camera orbit, marketing hero, invented confidence scores, or controls that pretend to execute infrastructure actions.

## Design Principles

Keep the evidence beside the system. Preserve environment boundaries. Reveal only the selected replay time. Distinguish rationale, observation, and confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls and entities, reduced motion, responsive layout, flat alternative and no-WebGL fallback.
